# SportsCenter — Product Vision

## One-line vision
SportsCenter is a personalized, spoiler-free sports-highlight DVR for Google TV: it determines which completed sporting events the user cares about, finds trustworthy official/authorized highlight videos, retains them in a rolling library, and makes them effortless to watch in a lean-back TV experience.

## Why this exists
The inspiration is the simplicity of XITE: open the TV app, make very few decisions, and content starts. General YouTube browsing has too much noise; traditional IPTV/media-center products solve different problems. SportsCenter should answer one question immediately: **What sports highlights worth watching are waiting for me?**

## Product principles
- TV-first, remote-first; D-pad navigation and couch-distance readability are first-class.
- Personalized, not comprehensive.
- Event-first discovery: sports data determines what happened; YouTube is a last-mile video source.
- Trusted sources only; prefer unavailable over questionable content.
- Spoiler-free by default.
- Lean-back playback through Catch Me Up and continuous queues.
- Fresh but forgiving: a rolling week, not a one-day news feed.
- Thin TV client: no media server, transcoder, DVR, or generalized media center.

## What SportsCenter is not
- Not IPTV/EPG, a general YouTube client, or a scores-first news app.
- Not a replacement for TiviMate/Jellyfin/Kodi.
- Not a repository/rehoster of video.
- Not a Movies/Shows/Live-TV hub in V1.

## North-star experience
Open SportsCenter after dinner. Home says there are new unwatched highlights and offers **Catch Me Up — ~45 min**. Press OK. SportsCenter plays a curated sequence of official highlights matching the user's interests. Next starts automatically. Scores were not spoiled. Back returns to the exact place in the TV UI.
